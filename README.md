# Unlockable Races of Tamriel (OpenMW)

Unlock Tamriel Data races by meeting them in person first. Another reason to finally go to the Mainland.

This mod is heavily inspired by Varlothen's [Hidden Powers - Unlockable Races of Tamriel](https://www.nexusmods.com/morrowind/mods/58172) (and my unfinished and unreleased OpenMW patch for it).

Ever needed an additional incentive to go exploring the Mainland?  
Does the sheer variety of options cause you choice paralysis?  
Or maybe you just want some meta-progression to spice things up?  
Well, I've got you covered!

Now, by default you won't have any TD races available for chargen - they will need to be unlocked first by meeting them in-game. In the settings you can specify what can be meant by "meeting them":

- Asking them about their race
- Talking to them
- Being greeted by them
- Checking their corpse

## Compatibility

Compatible with any race rebalances.

Compatible with any new race mods - they are unaffected by default. This can be changed in the settings.

Compatible with TD race unlockers, though there's not much sense in having them - this mod will override them.

Safe to install, update, or remove mid-playthrough. Removing the mod will revert all its changes.

## Requirements

- OpenMW 0.52 Nightly or newer
- [Tamriel Data](https://www.nexusmods.com/morrowind/mods/44537) - the mod was designed around it, but it's optional
